# PART 1 — DRAWING ANALYSIS

## 1.1 Scope and method

All 23 sheets were read individually at full resolution; elevations, sections and dimension plans were
re-rendered at 400–500 dpi and cropped so level tags and dimension strings could be read as written.

The set is **not** a new-build design. It is a refurbishment and fit-out package for an existing
three-storey building, drawn over a survey. Every plan legend distinguishes existing wall, proposed
extension wall, demolish-or-reduce beam drops, proposed beam drops, existing column, proposed column.

The set contains **no site plan, no roof plan, no structural grid, no finishes schedule and no overall
building envelope dimension.**

# PART 2 — RECONSTRUCTION RECORD (PARTS A–G)

## PART A — Final coordination decisions

### A. Front parapet: +38'-3" or +39'-3"
**Decision: +39'-3" everywhere.** Three independent sources say +39'-3" — the level ladder on A-201
itself, the body tag on A-202, and the body tag on A-203. Only the body tags on A-201 say +38'-3".
The ladder is controlling: it is dimensioned (4'-0" above roof at +35'-3"), identical on all four
elevation sheets, and agrees with both sections. One tag against five agreeing sources is a tag error.

### B. Courtyard: 28'-4", 28'-8" or 28'-10"
**Decision: 23'-0" × 28'-4", the ground floor value.** Measured wall-to-wall off A-111 the drawn
courtyard is 22.7' × 28.0', matching the ground floor tag within line weight. Ground floor is also
where the courtyard is a real floor rather than a void label. Modelled as one constant void from
+0'-6" to open sky, glazed wall 34'-0" high per A-301.

### C. West wing levels and "from courtyard level"
**Decision: read as absolute levels from road datum.** +13'-11" and +16'-11" sit 1'-6" and 4'-6"
above the main first floor; +25'-9" sits 1'-11" above the main second floor — plausible internal
steps. Read from the courtyard at +0'-6" instead, the +16'-11" pocket's 7'-10" headroom would
conflict with the slab above. Section B-B shows the west workstations at +2'-0", +12'-5", +13'-11",
+23'-10" and +28'-6" on one cut, all from the same datum as the rest of the section. **Five distinct
floor datums modelled.**

### D. Schedules versus elevation openings
**Decision: elevations govern external openings; schedules govern types and sizes.** 58 distinct
openings detected across the four elevations, 96 glazing panels placed. The scheduled glazed types
(GDW families) are named against meeting rooms, huddles, bathrooms and stores — **internal**
partitions — so they were not modelled as external openings.

Independent confirmation: first floor openings return z = 13.36'–20.42' from the elevations, against
a predicted 13.42'–20.42' from the stated 12'-5" FFL + 1'-0" sill + 7'-0" pane. **0.06 ft agreement.**

### E. Asbestos sheet roof versus RCC slab
**Decision: RCC slab everywhere at +35'-3", with corrugated sheet modelled over the east zone only**
(X 95'–151', Y 8'–95', matching the masjid / recreational area / rooftop cafeteria on A-113). The
sheet sits on the slab rather than replacing it — the conservative reading, so no structure is removed
on the strength of an undrawn boundary.

### F. Document control (not geometry)
Raise with Ingenious Design Studio: elevation names one sheet out of step in the register; door
schedules A-601/602/603 vs A-401/402(A)/403 colliding with the furniture plans; Section A-A listed as
5-301. The model follows the sheet titles, not the register.

### How each element was derived

- **Floor plates** from the reflected ceiling plans (A-501/502/503). The grey ceiling fill is a direct
  read of enclosed floor area per storey — better than closing wall lines broken by every door. Each
  ceiling sheet was registered onto its dimension plan by multi-scale template matching on the shared
  wall layer, scoring 0.92 / 0.92 / 0.85. The sheets are plotted at different zooms (1.025, 1.08, 1.08).
- **Walls** from the dimension plans. Existing walls are cyan, new/extension walls solid black; the two
  were separated by colour, the black layer filtered by thickness to drop text and dimension lines, and
  the union rasterised at 6". Envelope vs partition separated by eroding the floor plate (outer 9" band).
- **Monotonic stack.** Each lower plate is unioned with the one above; upper floors snapped out to the
  floor below where within 3'-0". Without this, registration slivers become floating slabs and false
  3-ft parapets across the plan. This is the one deliberate simplification, and it errs towards
  continuous structure.
- **Roof levels follow from the stack:** GF but not FF → +12'-5" (entrance foyer, guard); FF but not
  SF → +23'-10"; everywhere else → +35'-3". This produces the stepped skyline from geometry.
- **Openings** detected as rectangles on each elevation, de-duplicated across nested double-line
  outlines, converted to feet against that sheet's ladder, placed on the outermost wall plane of the
  correct storey, and the wall split into up to four solid pieces around each — real voids, not decals.
- **Stairs** as real stepped geometry at 5¾" riser / 1'-0" tread in dog-leg flights with mid-landings:
  main, west block, external service, and the south-east spiral.

### What is genuinely verified

The vertical dimension is the strongest part of this model. The level ladder is repeated on six sheets
and agrees with itself everywhere; the model reproduces 11'-5" floor-to-floor and 10'-8" clear on all
three storeys; and the window heads and sills derived independently from the elevations land within
0.06 ft of what the schedules predict. That cross-check was not built in — it fell out.

Horizontal geometry is **calibrated rather than dimensioned**. Good enough for area take-offs,
adjacency checks and visualisation. **Not good enough to order anything from.**

### What is not verified, in order of importance

1. **The absolute size of the building.** Everything rests on the calibration. One stated overall
   dimension converts the whole model from calibrated to dimensioned — a five-minute tape measurement.
2. **Horizontal positions of facade openings.** Right storey, right count, right size; position along
   each facade is proportional.
3. **The sheet-roof boundary.** Modelled conservatively as an overlay on a continuous slab.
4. **West wing zone boundaries.** Levels stated; extents read from the nearest wall line.
5. **Orientation.** No north point in the set. South-facing is an inference from the entrance, lawn,
   guard post and east service doors.
